Fear & Greed Index

A snapshot rebuilt each weekday after the US market closes. Ten categories (sentiment, volatility, positioning, trend, breadth, momentum, liquidity, credit, macro, cross-asset) are scored 0–100 from public data (Yahoo Finance, CBOE delayed options, CNN’s Fear & Greed Index, the AAII survey) and combined by the weights shown. A category whose data could not be fetched is marked unavailable and left out. Informational only, not investment advice.
